Why Tomato Prices Differ Between Markets

Tomato prices can fluctuate significantly between different markets due to several factors:

  • Arrivals and Supply: A market with a sudden surplus of tomatoes will likely see lower prices.
  • Local Demand: Demand from local consumers, restaurants, and processing units can influence prices.
  • Transport Infrastructure: Efficient transport reduces costs, potentially allowing for better farmer prices.
  • Storage Facilities: Availability of cold storage can help stabilize prices by managing supply.

How to Choose the Best Market to Sell Your Tomatoes

The "best" market isn't always the one with the highest quoted price. You need to consider your net profit:

Net Profit = Market Price - Transport Cost - Other Charges

Fasalo helps by comparing nearby markets and factoring in the estimated transport cost from your location, giving you a clearer picture of your potential earnings.

Tomato Price Trends in Maharashtra

Maharashtra is a significant producer of tomatoes. Prices often see high volatility, especially during seasonal gluts or supply shocks. Major supply centers include Nashik, Narayangaon (Pune district), and Pune APMC.

Tomatoes do not have a government-set Minimum Support Price (MSP) as they are a horticultural crop, making market intelligence even more critical for farmers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the best market to sell my tomatoes?

Enter your location, the quantity of tomatoes you want to sell, and your planned selling date in Fasalo. The tool compares nearby market prices and estimates your transport cost to recommend the market where you will earn the most after all costs.

Why do tomato prices vary between different markets?

Tomato prices differ between markets based on how much produce arrives that day (arrivals), local buyer demand, cold storage availability, and distance from major production areas. A market with fewer arrivals and strong buyer demand will typically offer a higher price.

What are the primary tomato markets in Maharashtra?

Major supply centres for tomatoes in Maharashtra include Nashik, Narayangaon (Pune district), and Pune APMC. These markets often see high trading volumes and can influence regional prices.
