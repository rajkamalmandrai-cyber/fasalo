How Fasalo Works: AI-Powered Market Recommendations Explained

Fasalo simplifies the complex process of selling crops by providing data-driven recommendations. Our tool helps you understand market dynamics and make informed decisions.

Our Process: From Your Farm to the Best Market

1. Input Your Details

You provide your location, the crop you want to sell, the quantity, and your preferred selling date. This information is crucial for personalized analysis.

2. AI Market Analysis

Our AI engine processes available market data, including historical trends and current price information (sample data for demonstration), for your chosen crop across various markets.

3. Smart Transport Estimation

We estimate the transport cost from your location to potential markets. This is a critical step, as the highest price market isn't always the most profitable after accounting for logistics.

4. Maximum Profit Recommendation

Fasalo then calculates the net profit for each market (market price minus transport cost) and recommends the best option to help you maximize your earnings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fasalo and how does it help farmers?

Fasalo is an AI-powered market intelligence platform that helps Indian farmers find the best market to sell their crops. It compares market prices, estimates transport costs, and provides personalized recommendations to maximize profit.

How does Fasalo calculate the 'best market' recommendation?

Fasalo's algorithm takes into account several factors: the market price for your specific crop and variety, the estimated transport cost from your location to various markets, and historical trends. It then calculates the net profit for each potential market to recommend the most profitable option.

Is the price data on Fasalo real-time and government-verified?

The price data on Fasalo is for demonstration and comparative guidance only. While we use publicly available market data, it is currently static/demo and does not represent real-time or government-verified market prices. Users should always verify current rates at their chosen market.
