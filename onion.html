Why Onion Prices Differ Between Markets

Onion prices can vary significantly between different markets due to several factors:

  • Local Supply (Arrivals): A sudden influx of onions in a market can depress prices.
  • Demand from Consumers and Traders: Local consumption patterns and demand from bulk buyers influence rates.
  • Storage Capacity: Availability of cold storage facilities can help manage supply and stabilize prices.
  • Transportation Costs: The cost of bringing onions from farms to markets impacts the final price.

How to Choose the Best Market to Sell Your Onions

The "best" market for onions isn't always the one with the highest quoted price. You need to consider your net profit:

Net Profit = Market Price - Transport Cost - Other Charges

Fasalo helps by comparing nearby markets and factoring in the estimated transport cost from your location, giving you a clearer picture of your potential earnings.

Onion Price Trends in Maharashtra

Maharashtra is the largest onion-producing state in India, with Lasalgaon in Nashik district being Asia's largest onion market. Prices here often set the trend for the entire country.

Onions are typically harvested in three seasons: Kharif (October-November), Late Kharif (January-March), and Rabi (March-June). The Rabi crop is the largest and has good storage quality, influencing prices throughout the year. Fasalo can help you track these trends and make timely selling decisions.

What are the main onion seasons in India?

There are typically three onion crops: Kharif (harvested Oct-Nov), Late Kharif (harvested Jan-Mar), and Rabi (harvested Mar-Jun). The Rabi crop is the largest and can be stored, influencing prices throughout the year.
