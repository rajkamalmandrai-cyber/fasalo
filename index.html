FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the market data accurate?

Yes, all our data is sourced directly from official Government of India APMC (mandi) portals in real-time to ensure the highest accuracy.

How is the 'Estimated Net Profit' calculated?

We calculate the total estimated value of your crop based on the live market price, and then subtract our estimated transport costs from your location to the mandi. This gives you a clear picture of your potential earnings.

Which languages are supported?

Currently, KrishiMitra is available in English, Hindi, and Marathi. We are actively working on adding support for more regional languages.
